### what does this little thing even do?

- **Live Discord Status & Vibe:** stalks my current status in real time via Lanyard so you know if i'm actually awake, idling, or actively avoiding my responsibilities.
- **Now Playing / Spotify Tracker:** publicly exposes whatever weird song or game i've been hyperfixating on for six hours straight.
- **The Heart Button:** clicky heart reaction button backed by a Cloudflare Worker for free, instant dopamine.
- **Page View Counter:** counts how many people wandered onto my profile page by accident.
- **My Links & Safe Spots:** pretty buttons to my guestbook, email, spotify, telegram, and throne wishlist so u don't have to look for them.
- **Mobile-Friendly Outfit:** because apparently people browse websites from their little rectangular pocket devices now.
